Information

Sharefolio supports listed securities traded on the NYSE and NASDAQ in the US, the ASX in Australia, the TSX in Canada, the TWSE in Taiwan and the OTC Market in Taiwan. The supported currencies include USD, AUD, CAD and TWD. If you wish to record an unlisted asset, you have an option to select 'OTHER' as the exchange, as long as it is traded in one of the supported currencies.

If price or FX data is unavailable, the asset's market value will default to your original purchase cost until updated data becomes available. All other returns generated from incomes such as dividends, distributions and interests are calculated as usual.

If a transaction has an impact on the quantity of your holdings as a result of buying, selling or adjustments (stock split, consolidation, etc.), it is generally considered a trade. On the other hand, if a transaction involves cash settlements only (dividends, distributions or interests), it is generally considered an income.

A dividend/distribution reinvestment transaction has two components: an income and a trade. In this transaction, you receive an income from your investment and use that amount to acquire more investments. On Sharefolio, when you add an income, you can elect to enter a dividend/distribution reinvestment trade on the same page. Alternatively, you can record the income and the reinvestment trade separately on the Incomes and Trades Pages.
