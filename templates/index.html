International Markets

Sharefolio supports securities traded on the ASX, NYSE, NASDAQ, TSX, TWSE and Taiwan OTC markets.

Listed & Unlisted Securities

Sharefolio automatically fetches price data for listed securities. What's more, you can also bookkeep unlisted securities & assets.

Auto FX Conversion

Sharefolio supports assets traded in AUD, USD, CAD and TWD. You can track your portfolio in asset currencies and your base currency without needing to convert currencies manually.

Easy Insights

Sharefolio analyses your portfolio automatically based on the transactions you provide, so you don't have to work out the maths yourself!
